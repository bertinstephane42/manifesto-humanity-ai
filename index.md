## Participate

You don't need to be an AI expert.

You don't need an academic title.

You don't need to represent an organization.

You can simply read, discuss, criticize, translate, improve or share the Manifesto.

### **Read. Discuss. Improve. Translate. Share.**

---

## The founding principle

> **Technological power must remain at the service of human dignity, freedom, truth, Goodness, and the Common Good.**

---

### Open project

This manifesto belongs to no party, religion, company, state or organization.

It is intended to be read, copied, shared, translated, printed, taught, discussed, criticized, adapted and improved.

**A free text for a free humanity.**
