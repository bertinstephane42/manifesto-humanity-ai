# Manifesto for Humanity in the Age of Artificial Intelligence

## Ten principles for keeping technological power at the service of human beings

**What should humanity do with its power?**

Artificial Intelligence may become one of the most transformative technologies in human history.

It can reduce suffering, accelerate scientific discovery, improve medicine, expand access to knowledge and increase human capabilities.

But technological power without a compass can also amplify our weaknesses.

This manifesto proposes ten principles for ensuring that artificial intelligence remains **at the service of humanity — and never becomes its master.**

---

## 🌍 A manifesto for everyone

You do not need to be an AI expert.

You do not need an academic title.

You do not need to represent an institution, a company or a political organization.

You only need to care about one question:

> **How can humanity remain human as artificial intelligence becomes more powerful?**

This is an **open, public-interest, non-partisan and non-religious project**.

It belongs to no party, religion, company, state or organization.

It is offered to everyone.

---

# The Ten Principles

### I — The Human Person Is the End

Human beings must never be reduced to data, profiles, scores, probabilities or economic value.

**Human dignity must remain the purpose of technological progress.**

### II — Goodness Must Guide Power

We must not ask only whether a technology is possible or profitable.

We must also ask:

> **Is it good? Who does it serve? Who might be sacrificed?**

### III — Artificial Intelligence Will Be an Instrument, Never an Idol

AI may calculate, advise, simulate and assist.

But computing power does not create moral authority.

> **The tool must never become the end.**

### IV — Humanity Must Not Abandon Its Intelligence

AI should help us learn and think — not make learning and thinking unnecessary.

> **Never automate a human faculty without preserving the ability to exercise it.**

### V — The Body, Privacy and Dignity Are Not Commodities

Technological progress must not turn human beings, their bodies, their intimacy or their suffering into commodities.

> **Technical progress that degrades human beings is not necessarily human progress.**

### VI — The Singularity Will Not Eliminate Human Responsibility

Even if artificial intelligence eventually surpasses humans in many capabilities, responsibility cannot simply be transferred to machines.

> **Humanity must never build a power that it is incapable of governing.**

### VII — We Have a Debt to Future Generations

The decisions we make today may affect generations that cannot yet participate in them.

> **We do not merely inherit the future: we are temporarily responsible for it.**

### VIII — Truth Will Never Be Replaced by an Answer

A convincing answer is not necessarily true.

A simulation is not reality.

A prediction is not certainty.

Generated content is not evidence.

> **Recognized uncertainty is better than manufactured certainty.**

### IX — Human Freedom Is Non-Negotiable

Algorithms must not silently determine what people see, desire, think or become.

> **Technology may know our behaviors; it must never possess our consciousness.**

### X — Power Must Remain Under the Law of the Common Good

Artificial intelligence cannot be governed solely by markets, governments, engineers or investors.

Technological power must remain accountable to humanity.

> **True civilization is not the one that can do everything, but the one that knows what it must not do.**

# An Open Text

This manifesto is not presented as the final answer.

No generation possesses all the wisdom necessary to foresee the consequences of artificial intelligence.

The text must therefore remain open to:

- criticism
- discussion
- translation
- adaptation
- improvement
- contribution

**Read it. Challenge it. Improve it. Translate it. Share it.**

---

# The Question We Leave to Humanity

The most important question may not be:

> **“Will AI become more intelligent than humanity?”**

It may be:

> **“Will humanity know how to remain human when an artificial intelligence becomes more powerful than it is?”**

And ultimately:

> **“What must we love, protect and pass on?”**
