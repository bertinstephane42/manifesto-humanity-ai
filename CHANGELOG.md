### Philosophy

The project establishes the manifesto as a public-interest text intended
to be freely shared, translated, discussed, adapted, and improved.

The manifesto is intended to remain independent of any political,
religious, commercial, or institutional ownership.

The manifesto text is intended to be dedicated to the public domain
under CC0 1.0 Universal, to the extent permitted by law.
